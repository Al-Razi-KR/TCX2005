## 1. This Week's Lecture: L5 (Self-Study)

This week we have one self-study lecture, **L5: IS Posed Challenges and Issues**. Please go through the videos & slides at your own pace before Thursday.

The key slides to focus on are:

- **Strategic IS Challenges** — understand how strategic systems create deep organizational change, affecting behavior, business processes, and cross-departmental coordination. The ComfortDelGro timeline is a good concrete illustration of what delayed Business-IT alignment looks like in practice.
- **Ethical and Social Issues** — the five moral dimensions of IS (information rights, property rights, system quality, quality of life, accountability), the five-step ethical analysis process, and the key technology trends that generate new ethical tensions (computing power, data analysis, networking, mobile devices).
  - **Example: NORA** — understand what it does and why it sits at the intersection of security benefits and privacy concerns. The comic strip on slide 20 captures the core tension well.
- **Legal Frameworks** — Singapore's Computer Misuse Act (CMA 1993) and the Cybersecurity Act, including the critical sectors.

The "Your Smartphone: Big Brother's Best Friend" case study is worth spending time on. It ties the ethical and legal content together in a practical way. The Match It activity (slides 36–38) is a useful self-check before Thursday.
