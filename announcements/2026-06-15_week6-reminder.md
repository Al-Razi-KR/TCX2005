This week's lecture, **L8: Securing Information Systems**, is pre-recorded. Please go through the videos and slides at your own pace before Thursday's session.

The key areas to focus on are:

- Understand the range of threats across the layers of an IS (client, communication lines, servers, and corporate systems), from hardware and software problems to human errors and network accessibility issues (slides 10–11).

- Understand the five control strategies: **Defense, Transference, Mitigation, Acceptance, and Termination**. Be able to apply these with examples (slide 24).
